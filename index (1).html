How to Use the CommNS Email Signature Builder

Step 1: Create Your Signature

  1. Enter your information in the input fields above.
  2. Select your preferred signature design from the options above. Double-check that all the contact information you want displayed is included and accurate.
  3. Once you're satisfied with your signature, click Copy formatted signature beneath your chosen design.
  4. Paste your signature into your email platform's signature settings. Instructions for Microsoft Outlook are provided below.

Step 2: Add Your Signature to Microsoft Outlook

  1. Open Microsoft Outlook.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Accounts → Signatures. In some versions, this may be under Mail → Compose and reply.
  3. Click Add Signature to create a new signature, or select Edit to update an existing one.
  4. Enter a name for your signature.
  5. Paste the signature you copied from the CommNS Signature Builder into the signature editor.
  6. Under For New Messages, select your newly created signature to make it your default.
  7. Under For Replies/Forwards, select your signature if you'd like it to appear in replies and forwarded emails.
  8. Click Save.

Step 3: Confirm Your Signature

Congratulations! Your CommNS email signature is now saved.

Send yourself a test email to confirm that your signature displays correctly, including all contact information, formatting, and logos. You're all set!
